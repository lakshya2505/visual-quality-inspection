⚡ 2D FFT & Spatial Frequency Analysis Studio

Image Processing Theory
The system decomposes spatial defect regions into 2D Fourier spectral basis functions: F(u, v) = \sum\sum f(x, y) e^{-j 2\pi (ux/M + vy/N)}. Sharp discontinuities (cracks, scratches) concentrate high-frequency energy at outer radii (r \ge 0.3 \cdot r_{max}), while smooth surface textures concentrate near the DC center.
